What's special

This project involves development of the Sofitel Hotel in the West Bay area of Doha, Qatar. The hotel is located in front of City Center Doha Mall, offering access to the capital's main commercial and shopping districts, and is designed to reflect a modern and contemporary aesthetic. The property will offer a wide range of restaurant and lounge concepts, a rooftop bar with panoramic city views, tailor-made meeting facilities, swimming pools, a SO Fit and a SO Spa. Tower 2 — Sofitel Hotel (5-star) — will be operated by Accor and stands 203 m tall with 3 basements, ground floor, mezzanine and 47 floors (including MEP). Parking provides 350 spaces in the basement and 55 on the ground floor. The tower has a total GFA of 37,805.5 sqm on a plot of 5,320 m² with 426 rooms in total.
